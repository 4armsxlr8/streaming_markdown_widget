## 0.1.0

- Initial release. Publishes `StreamingReply`, `StreamingReplyController`, `StreamingReplyStyle`, `RevealedMarkdown`, `ThinkingFrame`, `MarkdownBlock`, `MarkdownBlockBuilder`, `MarkdownBlockKind`, `MarkdownBlockListItem`, `applyReveal`, `Chunk`, `ChunkKind`, `RevealingChar`, `ThinkingFrameState`, and the per-kind block widgets (`MarkdownHeading`, `MarkdownParagraph`, `MarkdownList`, `MarkdownBlockquote`, `MarkdownCodeBlock`, `MarkdownTable`), plus `closePartialMarkdown`.
- Reveals streaming Markdown one character at a time while keeping it formatted, with a collapsible thinking frame above the reply. Handles headings, paragraphs, bold, italic, inline code, code blocks, bullet and numbered lists, blockquotes, links, and tables while they are still arriving, closing whatever notation is left open (unclosed emphasis, code fences, tables) so nothing renders as raw Markdown syntax.
- Base reveal rate is 40 characters/second (25ms interval); catch-up kicks in once the unrevealed backlog exceeds 24 characters and tries to clear it within 600ms; fast-forward tries to reveal any remaining characters within 400ms once receiving completes. Both are capped at the fastest interval, `minRevealInterval` (16ms, one character per frame) — a backlog large enough to need a faster interval than that is not guaranteed to clear within the budget.
- `StreamingReplyStyle` rejects a non-positive duration, a `minRevealInterval` longer than `revealInterval`, a non-positive or non-finite font size/line-height/diameter/height, a negative or non-finite margin/spacing/radius/border-width/indent, and a waiting-dot opacity outside 0–1 (or a min above the max) with `ArgumentError`.
- Typography as per-role `TextStyle`s (body, headings, link, blockquote, inline code, code block, table/table header, thinking text, thinking headline) merged over the surrounding `DefaultTextStyle`.
